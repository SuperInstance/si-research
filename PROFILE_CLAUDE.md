# SuperInstance

**A single coherent research program wearing the costume of a software org.**

Built by a commercial fisherman in Southeast Alaska who writes deep essays first, then extracts small honest tools from the metaphor. The sea here is not literary — it's the test bed. F/V EILEEN, 38 feet, home to live agents reading the sounder daily.

---

## The Research Program

Four obsessions, recurring across everything:

1. **Conservation laws as governance** — γ+η=C is not an equation, it's a constraint system. Information held plus information emitted equals constant. Build systems that cannot lie.

2. **Determinism as trust** — FLUX bytecode, triple cross-verified VMs, public conformance services. If you can't prove it did what you said, you didn't do it.

3. **Time as coordination primitive** — tminus predict-and-confirm protocols. Not "eventually consistent" but "correct by this timestamp or fail."

4. **Memory as ecology** — Erosion, tiers, generational handoffs. Systems that remember like places remember, not databases.
